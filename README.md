**PlateAndPost** is the commercial web platform built to help restaurant and cafe owners fix missing or low-quality dish visuals on **Zomato, Swiggy, and Instagram**—combining on-site food photography, branded posters, short-form video ads, and automated menu tracking.

> *"Every dish gets its photo."* — Sample restaurant agency offer & service showcase built with Google AI Studio, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, and Motion.

## What the Platform Covers

* **Problem Framing:** Highlights why restaurants with great in-person food lose online delivery orders when listings lack appetizing photos.
* **Before & After Showcase:** Interactive visual comparison demonstrating the lift from unstyled phone snaps to clean, delivery-ready dish photography.
* **Core Deliverables:** Full menu photo audit, on-site capture without kitchen disruption, branded posters & reels, and a Menu Photo Board tracker.
* **Transparent Sample Pricing & Booking Flow:** Sample package (`Rs 15,000 setup + Rs 3,000/month`) with direct WhatsApp and calendar booking CTAs.
